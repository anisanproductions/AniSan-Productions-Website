it was a thing of everyday
sleeping under the starry night
moonlight caressed you
your arms hugged the sky

but that was long ago
when loving wasn't an ache
why did it all have to change...

this set of memories
is your new set of life
the past is in the past
but your present 
you are at the sail

carry us to hope 

trying to squeeze some love out of her heart
knowing she won't succeed
but she must do it for her father and her 
they are all she has
