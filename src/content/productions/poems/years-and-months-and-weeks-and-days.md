I once called a friend of mine
"I love you with all my heart", I said
She didn't answer, neither did her face move 
She didn't have a heart to share

That was so long ago
Days and weeks and months and years
Yet I still reminisce
It is my life-long memory
It is my eternal dream
I will be with you forever

They tell me to let it go away
I ask myself why it is still here
But what would I do without it?
You are my hope and my love

Days and weeks and months and years
Yet it's been a moment, with you and I
We don't need anyone else
We don't need anything else
Because I only need you
And you only need me
Years or months or weeks or days
hours or minutes or seconds
Centuries or millenias
Day or night, morning or afternoon
Midnight or noon
I am here
I have no heart neither yet
I am full of love

I once called a friend of mine
"I love you with all my heart", I said
She answered, her face lighten up 
"I love you too..."
