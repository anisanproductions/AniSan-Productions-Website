No matter how much I tried
I couldn't believe his lies

I tried to imagine they tasted
like a sweet afternoon tea
or like a morning coffee
but they were a snake's venom
They tasted, smelled darkness
I tried to look at his eyes
I expected a lost, innocent
child's sad gaze
But his eyes were red
scarlet red
Red like the devil
Red of an evil heart
The red eye of the wind
I want to believe
I want to think they're truths
I want to believe he cares
I want to believe

I tried and tried
I tried to believe his lies
But I couldn't
I just can't
