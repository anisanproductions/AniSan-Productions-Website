Why are you so obsessed
over these worthless ideas?
Why do you spend the days
laying in bed lost in thought?
You won't find happiness there
Dreaming and analyzing 
won't make you smile again
Staring at the eye-eroding sun
won't bring you any hope

You say "Why do I feel always sad?
Why do I feel so empty and vane?"
Perhaps because you detached
Because you left the place of comfort
the true fountain of love and care

What's so hard to understand?
What makes your head clouded?
What covered your reasoning?
The solution is clear as water
The resolution, an easy of light
Don't follow the stars in the sky
Don't let your heart control

Follow the dazzling moonlight
Walk below and beside the moon
Look up to it like you used to
Hug it, kiss it, embrace it, touch it
Follow it until the end of your days.
