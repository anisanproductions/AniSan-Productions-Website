More than beautiful words
But still beautiful words
Beauty
on the top of a mountain
The ethereal
at the top of clouds
Heart-crushing,
mind-revealing,
kidney-extracting
Yet my hands
can't reach the sky
And my legs
can't climb mountains
I'm too blind
to see the other side
My eyes are weak
They cry yet the don't
They cry without tears
And I can't catch
any of them
Did I look wrong?
Did I choose wrongly?
Perhaps this wasn't
the love for me...
I lost the key
to the other side
The door might be
locked forever
And can it open again?
With force and despair
I kick the door
Frantically and with hope
I raise my fingers
to the sky
But the gates,
they remain closed
