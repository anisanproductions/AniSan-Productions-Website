i don't know if you remember
these things you used to feel
a mighty river that ended in the ocean
strong winds houses couldn't endure

i don't know if you remember
the flowers you used to plant
lilies next to the river
enjoying the warming sunset

i don't know if you remember
the tears of your beloved mother
nature herself has betrayed

i don't know if you remember
how easy it was to see
easy enviable ease and candle
words and letters carefully printed
with all the love of the heart

i don't know if you remember
no, i don't know if you remember
the sweet, tenderly blow of the sun
if you remember the comfy pillow

i don't know if you remember
you don't remember your heart
you don't remember your strings
you don't remember me
