I wish I had a twin sister
We would stay awake at night
and talk about the sky
We would share ideas
We could share our heart
She would hear the strings bowing
And she would understand
She would understand my core
And I would understand hers
We would always be together
Whether it be at the morning
During nighttime, on the screen
Each activity, each day
During waiting time, during the day
At nighttime
I wish I actually did
Because we would be the same
We would feel seen
It would always be a "we"
No need for "I" or "you"
We are happy
We did many things today
The world is so wicked
Too harsh for anyone alone
But us...
We would not need anyone
Because we'd have ourselves
She has me
I have her
