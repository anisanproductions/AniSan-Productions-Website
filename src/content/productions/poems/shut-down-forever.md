At least for me, it would not bring me any joy.
Any joy...

Is there something you don't understand?
Have your eyes been shut?

How does it feel?
That your life is merely a lie.

Is that so hard to understand?
Do you lack the understanding?
Maybe you shut your eyes forever, to never see again.
