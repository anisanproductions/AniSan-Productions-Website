To some, life is so simple
Out of the door, in of the house
To some, life is so easy
So crazy, so funny, a joke
To some, life never ends

Sing this through the wind
Swing your hopes away
To some, life is a traitor
To a few, life is a stone
Cold and calculated
Feelingless and mechanic
Bitter and unpredictable

Swing your hopes away
Swing your hopes up through
the air
Swing your warmth away
Let it fade as it flies up
