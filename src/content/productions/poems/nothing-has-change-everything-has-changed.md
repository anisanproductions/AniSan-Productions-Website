Sometimes I sit down, think,
check all my life until now,
and say "Nothing has ever changed.
Everything has been the same.

"Every time I sit down, think,
and check on life, the wind
is always blowing
to the same direction,
the Sun is shining
in the same way,
the clouds are moving
at the same pace,
the stars are waltzing
in the same dance,
and the Moon
is showing itself
in the same glory.

"Life is consistent.
If it hates you,
you will suffer
tragedy after tragedy,
hit after hit.
But if it loves you,
even your most
despicable sins
will be overlooked.

"Very consistent indeed.
Very unfair as well.

"The difference is,
life will always be there.
The clouds will always exist,
the wind will always blow,
the Moon
and the Sun
and the stars,
they will all outlive me.
But me,
who is a human
that just like it came,
just can it go...

"I'm just seated here.


"I wish I was Life.

"I wish clouds
did move for me
and stars
did waltz for me".
